# Community Code of Conduct

Legion contributors and users are expected to keep project spaces respectful,
welcoming, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific when reporting problems or reviewing work.
- Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, and technical opinions.
- Keep criticism about the work rather than the person.
- Protect private information and never pressure anyone to disclose credentials
  or personal details.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct
concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical.
